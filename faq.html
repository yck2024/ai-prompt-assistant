FAQ & support

Quick answers to common questions. Still stuck? Send us a note below.

Questions

Typing // does nothing. What should I check?
  • Make sure you're on a supported site and the cursor is in the message box.
  • Refresh the tab. Tabs that were open before you installed or updated the extension need a refresh.
  • Check that the extension is turned on at chrome://extensions.
  • On ChatGPT while signed in, version 3.2.2 has a known problem: the menu doesn't open. The next version fixes it.
I pressed Enter and my message was sent. How do I avoid that?

Use Tab to insert prompts. On many chat sites Enter sends the message straight away. Tab always inserts the prompt and leaves sending to you. On ChatGPT, the next version makes Enter insert too.

How does filtering work?

After //, keep typing. The menu shows shortcuts that contain what you typed, upper or lower case: //rev finds //reviseEnglish and //codeReview.

Can I use my prompts on another computer?

Yes, with Google Drive backup: upload on one computer, then download on the other. Or use Export & import to move a file across. Chrome's own sync doesn't copy prompts between computers.

After an update, the settings page opened and the sample prompts came back. Why?

After each install or update, the extension opens its settings page and adds back any of the five sample prompts that are missing. Your own prompts, and samples you edited, are kept as they are. You can delete the samples again.

How do I share prompts with my team?

Click Export JSON on the settings page and send the file. Your teammates click Import JSON to add those prompts to theirs.

Can I use it on a site that isn't listed?

Not in the Chrome Web Store version yet. Coming in the next version, Your Sites lets you add any chat site and allow access to it.

Does it work in other browsers or in desktop apps?

It's made for Google Chrome on the web. Some other Chromium browsers can install Chrome Web Store extensions, but Google Drive backup needs Chrome. It doesn't work inside desktop or mobile chat apps.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your prompts are stored in your browser, and we run no server and no analytics. Google Drive backup is optional and only touches its own file. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I remove everything?

Uninstall the extension to delete your prompts from the browser. If you used Drive backup, delete ai-prompt-assistant-prompts.json from your Drive and remove the extension's access in your Google Account settings.

Need help or have an idea?

Tell us through the feedback form. Bug reports, site requests and ideas are all welcome. It helps to include the site you were on, what you typed, and your Chrome version.
